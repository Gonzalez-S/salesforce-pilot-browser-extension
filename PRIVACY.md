# Privacy Policy — Salesforce Pilot (browser)

Salesforce Pilot is a browser companion for the Raycast Salesforce Pilot extension. It keeps a small amount of org metadata on your device so the two can work together when you open Salesforce.

## Data on your device

- Org identity details synced when you open an org from Raycast (such as username, org id, instance host, and display preferences)
- The Salesforce `sid` cookie may be read locally to match the current tab to a known org

## Data we do not collect

- Nothing is sent to our servers
- Nothing is sold or shared with third parties
- Sync metadata stays on your machine
